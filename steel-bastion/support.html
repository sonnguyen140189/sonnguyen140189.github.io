Playing online

  • Tap Online, choose 1 vs 1 up to 4 vs 4 and Create room. Send the 6-character code to your friends; they tap Online, enter the code and Join.
  • Empty seats are filled by bots. Everyone taps Ready, then the host taps Start.
  • Hold Hold to talk to speak to your team. Use Chat for quick messages.

Safety

  • Tap ••• next to a player in the room (or II › Players during a match) to Mute, Block or Report them.
  • Blocked players stay muted on your device in every room. Reports reach our moderation team with the recent chat of that player.

Ads and privacy

The game is free and shows one ad after a match. Change your ad consent any time in Settings › Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.
