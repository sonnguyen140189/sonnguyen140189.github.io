Steel Bastion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Tiếng Việt

Steel Bastion is made by SPARKHIVE TECH LTD ("we"), United Kingdom. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. Contact: skyline140189@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Settings (language, sound, graphics, blocked players) stay on your device.

Online play, chat and voice

Online rooms run on Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Germany). To connect you, Photon processes your IP address, a random session id, the room code, your in-game call sign (randomly generated, e.g. "Tank1234") and your game input.

  • Chat messages are relayed to the players in your room and are not stored by us, except when a player reports them (see below). Messages are filtered for offensive words.
  • Voice is sent in real time only while you hold the Talk button, only to players in your room (your team during a match). We do not record or store voice. The microphone is never used at any other time.
  • An anonymous player id (a one-way hash, not your device identifier) is shared with players in your room so they can block you on their device.

Reports

When you report a player, we store: the room code, your call sign and anonymous id, the reported player's call sign, the reason, that player's recent chat messages, the app version and platform. Reports are stored with Supabase (our database provider) and used only to keep the game safe. We keep them for 12 months.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd). AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads.

  • In the UK, EEA and Switzerland we ask for consent first (Google consent message). You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • On iPhone and iPad we ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.
  • Google's policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Legal bases (UK/EU GDPR)

Online play, chat and voice: performing the service you request. Reports and safety: our legitimate interest in keeping players safe. Personalised ads: your consent. Non-personalised ads: legitimate interest in funding a free game.

Children

Steel Bastion is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to or deletion of report data about you, or object to processing, by emailing us with your call sign and room code. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Data transfers and security

Our providers may process data outside your country (for example in the EU, US or Australia) under their own safeguards. Connections are encrypted where the provider supports it.

Changes

We will update this page if our data use changes and show the new date above.
